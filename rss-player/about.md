---
title: RSSPlayer
---

RSSPlayer turns RSS feeds into a video and audio player for iPhone and iPad. Paste
in a feed address and its episodes become a list you can play, queue, download and
cast. There's no account, no sign-up, and nothing to set up first.

## Feeds

- **Any RSS or Atom feed** that carries video or audio.
- **Rename feeds and put them in the order you want.**
- **Search inside a feed** to find an episode by name.
- **Nothing is fetched until you ask.** Feeds are read straight from whoever hosts
  them; RSSPlayer has no server in between.

## Playing

- **Video and audio**, with playback carrying on in the background and controls on
  the lock screen.
- **Picture in Picture**, so a video keeps playing while you use other apps.
- **AirPlay** to speakers, an Apple TV, or a smart TV.
- **It remembers where you stopped.** Every episode keeps its position, shows a
  progress bar, and is marked finished once you're near the end.

## The queue

- **Swipe to add** an episode to Up Next, or send it straight to the front with
  Play Next.
- **Plays straight through,** moving to the next episode by itself, and removing
  each one as you finish it.
- **Reorder or remove** anything in the queue, and select several episodes at once
  to queue, download, or mark as watched/unwatched.
- **Rewatching** restarts the progress bar so the queue does not accidentally skips the episode.

## Following shows

- **Follow a feed** for RSSPlayer to show you what's new since your last visit.
- **It checks in the background** a few times a day, as iOS allows, as well as
  whenever you open the app or pull down to refresh.
- **With Pro,** new episodes can notify you, add themselves to your queue, and
  download in the background, ready before you open the app.

## Casting to a TV

- **Cast to Chromecast and other Google Cast devices**, with the queue sent to the
  TV so it plays straight through there too.
- **With Pro, streams that a TV can't fetch can be relayed by your phone**, so shows that
  normally refuse to cast still play.
- **Your phone stays a remote:** play, pause, seek, skip, and volume.

## Downloads

- **Save episodes to watch with no connection**, including streams, which are
  saved as ordinary files.
- **Wi-Fi only if you'd rather**, and finished downloads can delete themselves.
- **Castable offline copies** keeps downloads in a form your TV can play from your
  phone.

Downloads are part of Pro.

## Privacy

Your feeds, queue, history and downloads stay on your device. There's no account
and no server of ours to send them to.
