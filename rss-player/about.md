---
title: RSSPlayer
---

RSSPlayer plays video and audio from RSS feeds on your iPhone and iPad.

- **Follow shows** and get a notification when a new episode appears.
- **Queue what's next** and play straight through.
- **Download episodes** to watch without a connection.
- **Cast to your TV**, with the phone relaying the video when a stream needs it.

RSSPlayer Subscription removes ads and lifts the limits on the queue and downloads.
