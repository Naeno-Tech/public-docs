**Last updated: 2026-09-**

RSSPlayer is made by Naeno Tech. This policy explains what the app collects, what it doesn't, and who else is involved.

Questions: **support@naeno.tech**

## The short version

RSSPlayer has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your feeds, your queue, your watch history and your downloads stay on your device. The free version shows ads from Google, which collect device information as described below. Paying for RSSPlayer Pro removes the ads.

## What stays on your device

The app saves all of this on your iPhone or iPad, and sends none of it anywhere:

- The feeds you add and the shows you follow
- Your queue, playback positions, and what you've watched or played
- Downloaded episodes
- Your settings

None of this is uploaded, backed up to us, or shared. It's included in your own iCloud or computer backups if you have those switched on, under your control and Apple's.

## Feeds you add

When you add or refresh a feed, or play an episode, your device requests it directly from whoever hosts it. Those servers can see your IP address, roughly when you made the request, and what you asked for, exactly as they would if you opened the same address in a web browser. We have no involvement in those requests and receive nothing from them. Those hosts have their own privacy policies.

## Advertising (free version only)

The free version shows ads through **Google AdMob**. To do that, Google's software collects information from your device, which may include:

- Your device's advertising identifier
- Device and app information, such as model, operating system version, and app version
- Approximate location derived from your IP address
- Interactions with ads, such as views and taps

Google uses this to choose and measure ads, including personalized ads where permitted. Google acts as an independent party for this, not on our behalf, and its handling of the data is covered by Google's own policies:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from partner sites and apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

**Your choices:**

- **App tracking:** the first time ads appear, iOS asks whether RSSPlayer may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, Google is not given your advertising identifier and ads are not personalized. You can change this any time in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking**.
- **In Europe, the UK and Switzerland:** a consent form appears before ads load, asking what Google and its ad partners may do. You can reopen it from **Settings → Privacy Options** in the app.
- **Remove ads entirely:** subscribe to RSSPlayer Pro.

## Purchases

RSSPlayer Pro is an auto-renewing subscription sold by Apple. Apple handles the payment and tells the app only whether a subscription is currently active. We never see your name, payment card, or billing address. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## Casting to a TV

When you cast, the app looks for devices on your local network and sends the video address to the one you pick. Some videos are sent from your device to your TV directly. Nothing about this leaves your network except the video request itself, which goes to whoever hosts the video.

## Notifications

Notifications about new episodes are created on your device by the app itself. No notification service or server is involved, and no device token is sent anywhere.

## Analytics

None. The app has no analytics, crash reporting or tracking of its own. If you send a crash report through Apple, Apple may share anonymized crash data with us; that is Apple's system and it is off unless you've turned it on.

## Children

RSSPlayer is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Feeds you add are chosen by you, and the app does not check what they contain.

## Keeping and deleting data

Since app data stays on your device, you delete it by deleting the app. You can also clear individual parts, such as watch history or downloads, in the app's settings. To ask Google to delete ad data associated with your device, use your advertising identifier controls and the links in the Advertising section above.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over personal data, including access, correction, deletion, and objecting to some uses. Because the app holds your data on your own device and we hold none, you can exercise most of these rights directly on the device. For data held by Google as an advertising partner, use Google's controls linked above. To ask us anything about this, write to **support@naeno.tech**.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising beyond the advertising described above.

## Changes
