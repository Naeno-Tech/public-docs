Questions, bugs or feature requests: support@naeno.tech

We usually reply within a couple of days. It helps to include your iOS version, your iPhone or iPad model, and, for a feed that won't play, the feed's address.

## Common questions

### An episode won't play. 
Some feeds link to a web page instead of a video file. RSSPlayer looks behind those pages for the video, but a few players hide it in a way the app can't reach. Send me the feed address and I'll take a look.

### My queue stops after five episodes. 
That's the free limit. RSSPlayer Pro removes it.

### I subscribed on another device. 
Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases.

### Cancelling
Subscriptions are managed by Apple, in the iOS Settings app under your name → Subscriptions.
