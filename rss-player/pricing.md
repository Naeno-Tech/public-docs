---
title: RSSPlayer Free vs Pro
---

RSSPlayer is free to use, with no account and no sign-up. Pro is an optional
subscription that removes the ads and lifts the limits.

## Free

Everything the player itself does is free:

- **Add any number of feeds.** Video or audio, no limit.
- **Follow shows** and see new episodes as they appear.
- **Cast to a TV**, use AirPlay, or watch in Picture in Picture.
- **Keep your place** in every episode, with playback continuing in the background
  and on the lock screen.
- **Queue up to 5 unfinished episodes.** Finish one and the slot frees up.
- **A short ad** plays when you add an episode to the queue, plus a banner at the
  bottom of the screen.

## Pro

- **No ads.** No banner, and nothing between you and the queue.
- **An unlimited queue.** Line up as many episodes as you like.
- **Downloads.** Save episodes to watch with no connection, as many as fit on your
  device.
- **New episode notifications** for the shows you follow.
- **Automatic queueing.** New episodes can be added to your queue by themselves,
  at the end or up next.
- **Automatic downloads.** New episodes can download in the background, ready
  before you open the app.
- **Auto-delete finished downloads,** so your storage looks after itself.
- **Castable offline copies,** which saves downloads in a form your TV can play
  from your phone.
- **Playback your way:** custom skip-back and skip-forward times, and control over
  how much of an episode counts as finished.

## Price

- **$1.99 per month**
- **$9.99 per year**, which works out at about 83¢ a month

Both plans are the same Pro. Switching between them is a change to your existing
subscription, not a second purchase. Pro is shared with your Family Sharing group,
so one subscription covers everyone in it.

Subscriptions are billed through your Apple Account and renew until you cancel.
Cancel any time from the iOS Settings app, under your name → Subscriptions.

## If you stop paying

Nothing is deleted. Your feeds, your queue and your downloads stay exactly where
they are and remain playable. Ads come back, and the limits apply again to adding
new things: you can't queue past 5 unfinished episodes or start new downloads until
you resubscribe.
